# Landing page brief

- Scope: a standalone web landing page in `landing/`; visitor mode is Persuade.
- Audience: Windows 10 and Windows 11 users who want a faster keyboard-first launcher and direct taskbar controls.
- Job: understand Prism in one viewport, inspect the current interface, watch the real workflow, and download the latest installer.
- Proof: the current launcher screenshot, the real product video, real settings captures, concrete supported workflows, MIT license, x64 support, current-user install, and no StartAllBack requirement.
- Constraints: do not change the Tauri application; do not invent metrics or testimonials; do not use generated or rendered product imagery; state the SmartScreen limitation; preserve keyboard and reduced-motion access.
- Direction: a bright Windows workspace pairs the rhythmic command headline, Find, Open, Control, with Prism's near-black interface. Black type, thin rules, and restrained violet establish the product without visual effects.
- Memorable moment: the three commands arrive in sequence and resolve beside the actual launcher before the visitor scrolls.
- Unresolved: production hosting and analytics are outside this build.
